Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: June 2025

1. Introduction

NestIQ Technologies Ltd. ("NestIQ", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

By using NestIQ, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Data Type What We Collect Why
Account Data Name, email, phone number, password (hashed) To create and manage your account
Profile Data Profile photo, role (tenant/owner), preferences To personalise your experience
Verification Data National ID / Passport image (optional) To verify your identity for trust
Listing Data Property details, photos, location To display your property to potential renters/buyers
Usage Data Pages visited, properties saved, search queries To improve the platform and AI matching
Device Data Browser type, IP address, OS For security and fraud prevention

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our platform.
  • To personalise property recommendations using our AI matching engine.
  • To send transactional emails (OTP verification, password reset, booking confirmations).
  • To verify user identity and prevent fraud.
  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To analyse usage patterns and improve our Service.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

NestIQ uses:

  • httpOnly Cookies: A secure, encrypted session token is stored in a browser cookie to keep you logged in. This cookie is not accessible by JavaScript (protecting against XSS attacks).
  • localStorage: Non-sensitive user information (your name, role) and your saved listing IDs are stored locally for performance. No passwords or tokens are stored here.
  • Theme preference: Dark/light mode setting stored locally.

5. Data Sharing

We may share your data with:

  • Google: If you use Google Sign-In, your Google profile data is shared with us per Google's OAuth terms.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Our database provider, hosted on secure cloud infrastructure.
  • Legal authorities: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of NestIQ and its users.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Property listing photos that you delete are removed from our servers immediately by our automated cleanup system.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt (salt rounds: 10)
  • JWT authentication via httpOnly cookies (not localStorage)
  • Rate limiting on login and OTP endpoints
  • HTTP security headers via Helmet.js (CSP, X-Frame-Options, etc.)
  • HTTPS encryption in production

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct: Update inaccurate data via your account dashboard.
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent: Opt out of non-essential data processing at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@nestiq.com.

9. Children's Privacy

NestIQ is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover that a minor has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a notice on the platform. Your continued use of NestIQ after the changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests:
Email: privacy@nestiq.com
